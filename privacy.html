Privacy Policy for Piano Timeline

Last updated: 27 June 2026

Piano Timeline (the "app") is an Android application developed by Keylight Piano for browsing and playing piano recordings hosted at timeline.keylightpiano.com. This policy explains what data the app and its backing server handle, and how.

Data the app collects on your device

The app does not ask you to create an account, sign in, or provide any personal information. It does not include any advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting services from third parties.

The following information is stored locally on your device only, and is never transmitted off the device:

  • The list of recordings (titles, dates, durations, file references) cached from the server
  • Your favorites, listening history flags, and playback preferences (shuffle, repeat mode)
  • Audio and MIDI files you have played, kept in the app cache so they don't need to be re-downloaded

Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app deletes all of this information.

Data the server receives

When the app fetches the recording list or streams an audio or MIDI file, it makes standard HTTPS requests to timeline.keylightpiano.com. As is normal for any web server, those requests are recorded in temporary access logs that contain the IP address of the connecting device, the timestamp of the request, the requested file path, and the user-agent string sent by the app. These logs are used solely for operating and securing the server (diagnosing errors, blocking abuse) and are not used to build profiles of users, sold, shared, or combined with any other data. They are retained for a short period and then discarded.

Data sharing with third parties

Piano Timeline does not share any data with third parties. The app communicates only with timeline.keylightpiano.com for the purpose of fetching recordings.

Distribution of the app through the Google Play Store is governed separately by Google's privacy policy; this is outside the developer's control and applies to any app installed from Google Play.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 18. It does not knowingly collect any information from children.

Security

All communication between the app and the server uses HTTPS (TLS) encryption. Because the app does not collect or transmit personal information, there is no personal data on the server side that could be exposed in the event of a breach.

Your rights

Since the app does not collect personal data, there is no personal data to request, correct, or delete. You can remove all locally-stored data at any time by clearing the app's storage in your device settings or by uninstalling the app.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent revision.
